# Multi-page Statement Reconciliation

Complete the supplied starting workbook from the two-page bank statement and save it as `/app/output/answer.xlsx` for cash-management review.

Extract every transaction with its debit or credit direction, keep the continuation page for each record, and produce a formula-linked reconciliation from the opening balance to the printed closing balance. Include a concise category summary and source traceability table. You may assign any stable unique transaction IDs. Category wording may vary when it preserves the same business meaning (for example, Receipts/Customer receipts or Fees/Bank fees). Any readable locator identifying the statement file, page, and transaction is acceptable.

The reviewer must be able to update a transaction amount and see the reconciliation respond.
