# Scenario definition

The base case uses the supplied 2023 coal, natural-gas, wind, solar, and demand inputs. `Other generation` is the residual between demand and those four listed categories and remains constant. The policy case displaces 8% of coal, increases wind by 5%, increases solar by 12%, and applies 1% demand growth. Natural gas balances the remaining policy demand. Apply 1.0 tCO2/MWh to coal and 0.4 tCO2/MWh to natural gas. Because generation is supplied in GWh, absolute emissions in metric tonnes equal `GWh × 1,000 × tCO2/MWh`.
