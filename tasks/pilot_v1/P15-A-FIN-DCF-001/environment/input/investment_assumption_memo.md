# Investment case assumptions

Forecast revenue growth declines from 6.0% in FY2025E to 4.0% in FY2029E. EBIT margin progresses from 31.5% to 32.4%. Use 21.0% cash tax, D&A at 2.9% of revenue, capex at 2.7% of revenue, change in NWC at 1.2% of revenue, 8.5% WACC, and 2.5% terminal growth. Use debt of $97,800m.
