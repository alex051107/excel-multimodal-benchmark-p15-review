# Receipt Batch Expense Reconciliation

Complete the supplied starting workbook from the three receipt images and save it as `/app/output/answer.xlsx` for the expense-management team.

Maintain each receipt's identity, merchant, date, item hierarchy, and source file. Classify the extracted expenses as Meals, Travel, or Office; calculate each receipt total including tax and tip where shown; and provide a batch reconciliation that a reviewer can trace back to the images. Receipt IDs may follow the images or another stable unique convention. Any readable locator that identifies the correct receipt image and item is acceptable.

Do not merge or silently discard records merely because their layouts differ.
