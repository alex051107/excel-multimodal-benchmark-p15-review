# Contractor Quote Normalization

Procurement needs a clean, editable comparison workbook from the attached contractor quotation scan. Complete the supplied starting workbook and save it as `/app/output/answer.xlsx`.

Capture the quote header, normalize every scope row, and keep the base scope distinct from the optional alternate. Calculate the base-scope total after the stated discount and tax; do not include the optional alternate in that total. Retain a short source-traceability table so a buyer can find every quoted line in the original scan. You may assign any stable unique line IDs and any readable locator convention, provided the IDs link consistently and the locator identifies the supplied scan and quoted line.

The workbook is for a purchasing review and must be understandable without reopening the image.
